# Auto-Renewal Rules

## Standard notice windows

Most vendor agreements renew automatically unless written notice of non-renewal or renegotiation is served before the notice deadline. The notice period is set by the contract; where the contract is silent, the following defaults apply:

- Software licences and SaaS subscriptions: 60 days before renewal.
- Annual maintenance contracts (AMC): 30 days before renewal.
- Cloud commitments and reserved capacity: 90 days before renewal.
- Subcontractor statements of work: 30 days before renewal.

The notice deadline is the renewal date minus the notice period. Notice served on or after the deadline is not effective for the current term.

## Review lead time

Every renewal must be reviewed at least 30 days before its notice deadline, so that there is time to benchmark pricing, consult the business owner and obtain the approvals required by the contract's value band.

A contract whose notice deadline falls within the next 30 days is approaching its window and must be prioritised in the current review cycle.

## Inside the notice window

A contract is inside its notice window once today's date is past the point where a full review can be completed before the notice deadline, but the deadline itself has not yet passed.

Contracts inside the notice window must be escalated to a named human approver regardless of value band, because negotiating leverage is reduced and the consequences of an incorrect decision are materially higher. Where the decision is to renegotiate or terminate, written notice must be served to the vendor within five business days of the decision.

## Missed notice deadlines

If the notice deadline passes without notice being served, the contract renews for a further full term on the vendor's proposed terms. The organisation loses its ability to exit or to credibly threaten to exit.

When a deadline is missed, the BizOps Manager must record the miss, notify the Procurement Head, and note the contract for review at the earliest point in the next term. Mid-term termination of an auto-renewed contract is governed by the early termination rules in the termination procedure and usually carries a penalty.
