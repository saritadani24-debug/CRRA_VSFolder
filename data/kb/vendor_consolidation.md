# Vendor Consolidation

## Identifying overlap

Two or more contracts overlap when they provide the same business capability, such as collaboration, project tracking, endpoint security or cloud hosting, to the same or substantially the same user population.

Overlap is assessed across the whole portfolio by contract category, not contract by contract. Every renewal review must check whether another active contract in the same category already provides the capability.

## Utilisation thresholds

Where overlapping contracts exist, the contract with the lower licence utilisation is the candidate for consolidation into the other.

A contract with utilisation below 40% that overlaps with another active contract should be recommended for consolidation. A contract with utilisation between 40% and 70% that overlaps should be reviewed with the business owner before a recommendation is made. Utilisation above 70% is generally healthy and does not by itself justify consolidation.

## Migration cost assessment

Consolidation is only recommended where the expected saving over the first two years exceeds the estimated migration cost, including data migration, retraining, integration rework and any overlap period where both contracts run in parallel.

Where migration cost cannot be estimated from available information, the recommendation must state that clearly and flag that migration cost may exceed first-year savings.

## Consolidation approval

A consolidation recommendation is approved at the band of the contract being retired, but the receiving contract's business owner must agree in writing to absorb the additional users.

If absorbing the users would increase the receiving contract's annual value enough to move it into a higher approval band, the consolidation must be approved at that higher band.
