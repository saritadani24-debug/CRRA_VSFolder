# Approval Thresholds

## Annual value bands

Every vendor contract is assigned an approval band based on its annual contract value (ACV) in Indian rupees, excluding GST.

- Band A: annual value below ₹10 lakh.
- Band B: annual value from ₹10 lakh up to and including ₹50 lakh.
- Band C: annual value above ₹50 lakh.

The band is determined by the value of the proposed renewal, not the value of the expiring term. Where a renewal quote moves a contract from one band to another, the higher band applies.

## Required approvers by band

Band A renewals may be approved by the BizOps Manager responsible for the vendor relationship, provided a business owner is on record for the contract.

Band B renewals require a Finance review of the commercial terms followed by written sign-off from the Procurement Head. Neither approval may be delegated to the requester.

Band C renewals require Finance review, Procurement Head sign-off and final approval from the CFO. A contract worth 60 lakh rupees per year is therefore a Band C decision and cannot be approved below CFO level.

No AI-generated recommendation substitutes for a named approver in Band B or Band C. A recommendation may inform the decision; the approver owns it.

## Multi-year commitments

For contracts with a term longer than twelve months, the band is set by the annual value, but the approver must also see the total contract value across the full term.

Any multi-year commitment whose total value exceeds ₹1 crore requires CFO approval regardless of its annual band. Prepayment of more than one year in advance requires Finance review in all bands.

## Splitting and delegation

Splitting a single requirement across several smaller contracts or purchase orders to keep each below a band threshold is prohibited. Contracts with the same vendor for the same capability are aggregated when determining the band.

Approval authority may be delegated only in writing, only to a person at the same or higher grade, and only for a defined period. Delegated approvals must name both the delegate and the original approver in the approval record.
