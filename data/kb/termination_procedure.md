# Termination Procedure

## Grounds for termination

A contract may be recommended for termination only when the capability it provides is no longer needed by the business, or when the vendor has materially failed to deliver against the agreement.

Low utilisation alone is not grounds for termination. Where the capability is still needed but is provided by another vendor, the correct recommendation is consolidation, not termination.

## Business owner confirmation

Every termination requires written confirmation from the contract's business owner that the capability is no longer needed, or that a replacement is in place. This confirmation must be attached to the approval record.

Where no business owner is on record for a contract, the contract cannot be terminated until one is identified and gives confirmation. The absence of an owner is not evidence that the capability is unused.

## Data retrieval and exit

Before notice of termination is served, the business owner and IT must confirm that all organisation data held by the vendor can be exported in a usable format, and must agree the export date.

The exit plan must cover data export, deletion certificates from the vendor, revocation of user access and API keys, and the transition of any dependent workflows. Termination notice must not be served until the exit plan is approved.

## Early termination and penalties

Termination before the end of a contract term usually incurs an early termination fee or the balance of committed fees. Early termination requires Finance review in all bands, and the penalty must be shown alongside the expected savings.

Early termination of a contract above ₹50 lakh annual value, or of any contract where the penalty exceeds ₹10 lakh, requires CFO approval.
